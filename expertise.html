Family Law

Family Law deals with legal matters involving family relationships and domestic issues. It helps individuals resolve personal and family disputes while protecting their legal rights and interests.

  • Divorce and separation: Assisting couples with the legal process of ending a marriage.
  • Child custody and maintenance: Helping determine parental responsibilities, child support and arrangements for children.
  • Adoption: Providing legal guidance on the process of adopting a child.
  • Domestic violence: Advising and assisting individuals seeking legal protection from abuse.
  • Property division: Helping resolve disputes over assets and property following a divorce or separation.

Criminal Law

Criminal Law focuses on offences against the law and the legal process of investigating, prosecuting and defending individuals accused of crimes.

  • Criminal defence: Representing individuals accused of committing criminal offences.
  • Court representation: Presenting arguments and evidence on behalf of clients in criminal proceedings.
  • Bail applications: Assisting accused persons in applying for release while awaiting trial.
  • Legal advice: Explaining the rights of accused individuals and guiding them through criminal proceedings.
  • Appeals: Challenging a conviction or sentence where there are valid legal grounds.

Labour/Employment Law

Labour and Employment Law governs the relationship between employers and employees. It aims to ensure that workplace rights and obligations are respected.

  • Unfair dismissal: Assisting employees who believe they have been dismissed unlawfully or unfairly.
  • Employment contracts: Advising on employment terms, responsibilities, salaries and working conditions.
  • Workplace disputes: Resolving disagreements between employers and employees.
  • Discrimination and harassment: Advising on legal issues involving unequal treatment, bullying or harassment at work.
  • Wages and benefits: Helping resolve disputes concerning unpaid salaries, leave, overtime and other employment entitlements.

Tenancy and Rental Law

Tenancy and Rental Law concerns the legal relationship between landlords and tenants. It helps both parties understand their rights and responsibilities when renting property.

  • Rental agreements: Advising on the terms and conditions of tenancy agreements.
  • Eviction matters: Assisting landlords with lawful eviction procedures and tenants facing eviction.
  • Rent disputes: Resolving disagreements involving unpaid rent, rent increases or payment conditions.
  • Security deposits: Handling disputes over the return or deduction of rental deposits.
  • Property maintenance: Advising on responsibilities for repairs, maintenance and the condition of rented premises.

Commercial Law

Commercial Law deals with the legal aspects of business activities and transactions. It helps businesses operate within the law and manage legal risks.

  • Business formation: Advising on the legal requirements for establishing and structuring a business.
  • Business transactions: Assisting with the legal aspects of buying, selling and transferring businesses or assets.
  • Corporate governance: Advising companies on compliance, management responsibilities and legal obligations.
  • Commercial disputes: Resolving disagreements between businesses, suppliers, customers and business partners.
  • Regulatory compliance: Helping businesses understand and comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Contractual Disputes

Contractual Disputes arise when two or more parties disagree about the terms, performance or interpretation of a legally binding agreement.

  • Breach of contract: Assisting clients when another party fails to fulfil its contractual obligations.
  • Non-payment disputes: Helping recover money owed under an agreement.
  • Contract interpretation: Clarifying the meaning of disputed contractual terms.
  • Damages and compensation: Advising clients on possible remedies for losses caused by a breach.
  • Dispute resolution: Negotiating settlements or representing clients in court proceedings where necessary.

Litigation and Arbitration

Litigation and Arbitration are methods used to resolve legal disputes. Litigation involves resolving disputes through the courts, while arbitration involves presenting a dispute to an independent arbitrator for a decision.

  • Litigation: Representing clients in civil or commercial court proceedings.
  • Case preparation: Gathering evidence, preparing legal documents and developing legal arguments.
  • Court representation: Presenting a client's case and responding to arguments from the opposing party.
  • Arbitration proceedings: Representing clients in disputes resolved by an arbitrator outside the ordinary court process.
  • Enforcement of decisions: Advising on the procedures for enforcing court judgments or applicable arbitral awards.

Mediation

Mediation is a form of alternative dispute resolution in which an independent and impartial mediator helps parties reach a mutually acceptable agreement. The mediator facilitates communication but generally does not impose a decision.

  • Family mediation: Helping family members resolve disagreements concerning separation, children or family arrangements.
  • Commercial mediation: Assisting businesses in resolving disputes while maintaining professional relationships.
  • Workplace mediation: Helping employees and employers address conflicts.
  • Negotiation support: Encouraging constructive discussions to identify practical solutions.
  • Settlement agreements: Helping parties document the terms of an agreed resolution, where appropriate.

Contract Drafting

Contract Drafting involves preparing clear, legally appropriate agreements that establish the rights and obligations of the parties involved. Well-drafted contracts help prevent misunderstandings and reduce the risk of future disputes.

  • Employment contracts: Preparing agreements that define job responsibilities, salary, working hours and other employment conditions..
  • Business agreements: Drafting agreements between business partners, suppliers, customers and service providers.
  • Rental agreements: Preparing tenancy contracts that explain rental payments, responsibilities and conditions.
  • Confidentiality agreements: Drafting agreements to protect confidential business information.
  • Review and amendment: Examining existing contracts, identifying risks and updating terms when necessary.
